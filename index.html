What is neurofeedback?

Your brain, learning to regulate itself

Neurofeedback is a non-invasive, evidence-based form of brain training. Sensors placed gently on your scalp measure your brainwave activity in real time. That information is fed back to you through a visual and/or auditory signal and your brain will begin learning optimal patterns for peak performance and regulation.

Over a series of sessions, your brain builds new, more balanced patterns that carry into your daily life. Just your brain’s own neuroplasticity, guided by real-time feedback.

Neurofeedback is a wellness service. It is not a substitute for medical or mental health treatment. Individual results vary.

Common questions

FAQ

How should I prepare for a session?

Come rested and well-hydrated. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior. Wash your hair the morning of your session and skip heavy conditioners or styling products, as these can affect sensor contact. Wear comfortable clothing and plan to relax.

Do you accept insurance?

Beach City Neurofeedback is a fee-for-service practice and does not bill insurance directly. Upon request we can provide a superbill you may submit to your insurer for potential out-of-network reimbursement. We make no guarantee of reimbursement.

Is this a medical or therapy practice?

No. Beach City Neurofeedback provides neurofeedback brain training as a wellness service only. We do not diagnose, treat, or provide clinical care for any medical or mental health condition. If you are experiencing a medical or mental health concern, please consult a licensed physician or mental health professional.
